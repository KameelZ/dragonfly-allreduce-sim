Course spec requirement: DF/DF+ builds should use 'roughly equal resources -- matched switch count, aggregated bandwidth, and link count; differences must be documented.'

Design decision: DF+ here always uses spines_per_group == leaves_per_group (the standard Dragonfly+ full bipartite leaf-spine fabric). We deliberately do NOT shrink DF+'s spine count to force switch-count parity with DF -- a single spine would funnel all intra-group traffic through one switch, which is a congestion confound worse than the resource-count delta documented below.
